RFI-PaCe

Team Australia · 6th IEEE GRSS Student Grand Challenge

We're a student team from Griffith University and the University of Queensland, selected as Team Australia for the sixth IEEE GRSS Student Grand Challenge. We're designing a PocketQube-class payload with separate X-band and K-band receivers that detects radio-frequency interference from orbit, characterises it, and maps it — processing on board so we downlink a compact record instead of raw signal.

The organisations below make that possible. Their support covers the software, hardware and expertise behind every board we build, and we're grateful for all of it.
